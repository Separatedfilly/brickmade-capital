Lead offer

Real estate consulting

For owners, principals, and GPs of investment and development firms. Operations is one cluster, alongside portfolio, capital, and deals.

Platform & operations

How the firm runs — systems, assets, leasing, and senior operating help, including the first months after a close.

Operating platform build

  • Role clarity and operating cadence
  • KPI dashboards that ownership actually uses
  • Budget rhythm and vendor stack cleanup

Asset management & NOI

Lease-up strategy, capital plans, and lender / investor reporting packages that hold up under refinancing.

  • NOI improvement plans with owner accountability
  • Capital planning tied to hold / exit goals
  • Institutional-grade reporting packages

Leasing strategy

Speculative and build-to-suit industrial; value-add repositioning; pre-leasing milestones ahead of delivery.

  • Leasing plans and milestone tracking
  • Broker / team coordination from the owner’s side
  • Value-add and repositioning leasing strategy

Fractional / interim COO

Part-time operating leadership for CRE firms that need senior ops ownership without a full-time seat.

  • Interim leadership through a growth or transition stretch
  • Hands-on accountability across ops, asset management, and finance touchpoints
  • Right-sized retainer after discovery

Post-acquisition / value-add ops takeover

Hands-on operating ownership for the first 90–180 days after close or into a value-add plan — systems, leasing, CapEx, and reporting stood up fast.

  • 90–180 day ops takeover after close
  • Lease-up, CapEx, and vendor rhythm from day one
  • Reporting and KPI cadence for ownership

Portfolio & capital

Decisions on the assets you already own — hold or sell, where capital sits, and reporting lenders and investors can stand behind.

Portfolio / asset review

Fixed-scope sprint on hold vs sell, CapEx need, lease-up risk, covenant pressure, and underperforming assets — ending in a prioritised action list for ownership.

  • Hold / sell and CapEx clarity by asset
  • Lease-up, covenant, and underperformance flags
  • Prioritised owner action list from a short sprint

Capital identification

Find trapped equity, refinance capacity, preferred equity / JV room, and disposition proceeds — often paired with a portfolio / asset review.

  • Trapped equity and refinance capacity maps
  • Preferred equity / JV room screening
  • Disposition proceeds paths tied to the asset plan

Investor & lender reporting rebuild

Cleaner packages ownership and lenders will actually use — cadence, structure, and narrative that hold up under refinance and LP scrutiny.

  • Reporting cadence and package structure
  • LP- and lender-ready narrative and metrics
  • Built for refinance and ongoing investor updates

Deals

Operator-led acquisition search and client-side underwriting for transactions the firm pursues.

Targeted acquisition search

Operator-led, mandate-driven deal search — not a broker desk — with underwriting screens and a shortlist that carries ops and capital notes.

  • Mandate-driven sourcing against your criteria
  • Underwriting screens before anything reaches ownership
  • Shortlist with ops and capital notes, not a listing dump

Capital & underwriting support

Client-side acquisition diligence, refinance packages, and disposition prep — financial fluency with operator accountability.

  • Diligence and underwriting support for your deals
  • Refinance and investor package prep
  • Disposition readiness without Brickmade taking principal risk

Brickmade Capital provides consulting services only. It does not buy, sell, or solicit interests in real estate, and does not provide legal, tax, or securities advice. Capital and underwriting support is advisory to the client’s own transactions.
